Our Mission

Built to protect the internet.

INFRAUD is an intelligence platform designed to democratize digital safety. We aggregate threat signals to provide explainable risk scores before you make a mistake.

The trust paradigm is broken.

Scammers are sophisticated, utilizing psychological manipulation and perfect visual replicas of trusted brands. The burden of security has unfairly fallen on the end-user.

By aggregating hundreds of threat signals—from domain infrastructure and SSL forensics to community-driven intelligence—we provide everyday internet users with instant, explainable risk assessments.

Our engineering principles

We build security tools differently. No black boxes, no hidden agendas. Just transparent, verifiable algorithms.

Radical Transparency

Security through obscurity is a failed model. Our detection algorithms and core heuristics are entirely open-source. Anyone can audit how we arrive at a verdict.

Privacy by Design

When you paste a suspicious URL into INFRAUD, we extract the indicators of compromise and immediately discard the payload. Queries are evaluated entirely in-memory.

Real-time Intelligence

Scammers adapt in minutes. Our global intelligence graph updates continuously, ensuring you are protected against the latest threats before they spread.
