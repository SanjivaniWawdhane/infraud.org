Citizen Safety Playbook (सुरक्षित राहण्यासाठी काय करावे?)
  • Check the address bar: Always verify that the domain name exactly matches the official company before entering any passwords.
  • Never enter OTPs or PINs: Authentic banks and payment apps will never send web links asking you to enter login OTPs or PINs.
  • Beware of fake urgency: Threatening messages like "KYC Expired" or "Account Blocked Today" are classic signs of online fraud.
  • Never download APK files: Avoid installing apps or software prompted by unfamiliar websites or popups.
