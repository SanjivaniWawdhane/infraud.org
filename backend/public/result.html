UPI Fraud Simulator: Common Trap Mechanics

🔄 Scam A: The Fake Collect Request Trap

Scammer sends a payment request on PhonePe/GPay saying "Approve to receive ₹5,000 refund/cashback".

🛑 NEVER ENTER PIN TO RECEIVE MONEY. Entering UPI PIN ALWAYS deducts money from your bank account!
📷 Scam B: The WhatsApp QR Code Trick

Scammer sends a QR code image via WhatsApp claiming "Scan this QR code in your app to receive OLX buyer money".

🛑 Scanning a QR code is ONLY for paying out. Money cannot be credited into your account by scanning a QR code!
